## Ordering (FAQ)
- No account needed: an email address is enough to order and track. An account keeps a wishlist and order updates.
- With an account you are enrolled automatically in Access, the loyalty programme, from the first order. Benefits mentioned: private sales, priority customer service, unlimited free delivery, personal stylist (as you move up).
- Order by phone is possible through customer service.
- You can cancel some items before the order is being prepared (Orders & Returns in the account, or order number + email for guests). You can't add items to a placed order: place a new order.
- Pre-orders: next season pieces before release; paid in full when ordering; some partners accept cancellation before preparation; shipped separately; some have quantity limits; refundable under the returns policy.
- No reservations: items can't be held.
- Out-of-stock size: "Size unavailable?" in the size menu, leave size and email to be notified.

## Pricing and payment (FAQ)
- Prices are set by each brand and partner boutique, so the same item can cost differently depending on where it ships from and your delivery destination. Cart or wishlist doesn't lock a price. Farfetch shows the best available price for your destination at order time. Select your delivery destination for accurate prices.
- Currency depends on the delivery destination, shown at checkout; if local currency isn't available, charged in USD.
- Debit card, crypto and PayPal are charged when the order is placed.
- Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express, Discover, Diners, JCB, PayPal, Apple Pay, Tamara; Afterpay (US and Australia only); Alipay (US, Canada, mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao); Klarna (US, UK, DE, AT, CH, BE, IT, FR, SE, NL); crypto (list on the crypto page); others local.
- Pay in instalments via Klarna, Afterpay, Tamara in some countries. Maximum spend: US $1,500; France €1,500; Belgium €5,000; UK £1,500.
- Security checks on all payments when the order is placed.

## Authenticity and condition (FAQ)
- Selection from the best luxury brands and boutiques worldwide; Farfetch guarantees authenticity.
- Packed in a protective Farfetch box; brand dust bags or boxes included when the brand provides them.
- Pre-owned conditions: Unworn with tags; Unworn (no tags); Excellent (little or no wear); Good (some signs of wear: light discoloration, patina, scratches).

## Delivery, duties, taxes (FAQ)
- Shipping cost depends on size, weight, destination; flat delivery cost above a threshold; items from several origins can ship for one delivery price; cost shown at checkout.
- Dispatch within 2 business days after validation; delivery usually 2–7 days after dispatch depending on location and method.
- Items from several boutiques arrive separately, each with tracking.
- DDP destinations include the US, Canada, UK, EU (except Canary Islands), Australia, Switzerland, Japan and more: applicable taxes included in the price paid, nothing more to pay on delivery.
- US: Farfetch does not collect sales tax in every state; your purchase may be subject to sales or use tax unless your state is exempt; many states require a yearly use tax return for untaxed purchases.
- Some items can't ship to some regions (brand choice; exotic materials or beauty items can be restricted by customs).
- No paper invoice in the box: digital invoice in Orders & Returns.

## Returns and refunds (returns page + FAQ)
- Returns accepted within 30 days from delivery (returns page). (The French FAQ says "130 jours": treat as a typo, use 30.)
- Items unworn, undamaged, unused, all original tags; brand boxes and dust bags included, protected inside the box. Badly packed returns may not be refunded. No exchanges.
- Final Sale items can't be returned unless damaged or faulty on arrival.
- Made-to-order items can't be returned.
- Free returns: courier collection or drop-off (options depend on location). Book collection within 7 days of receiving the order.
- Return from the country/region where you ordered.
- Refund: up to 6 calendar days to process once received and accepted; up to 14 days to appear, depending on payment provider. Refund to original payment method; outbound delivery cost not refunded (FAQ); DDP duties and taxes refunded through Farfetch.
- Option: Farfetch account credit (5-year expiry, non-transferable).
- Crypto orders: return within 30 days of order; refund by TripleA in the original crypto.
- Several boutiques: book one collection per return; each parcel separately with its own return label; commercial invoice (Return note) outside the box.
