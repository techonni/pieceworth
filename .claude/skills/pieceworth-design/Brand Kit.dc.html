05 · Voice

Like a friend who checked first.

Ask the real question

Headlines are the question the reader would type: “Is HEWI legit?”, “Will you pay import duties?”

Show the proof

Date checked, official source, “Affiliate link” next to every store link. Never a claim we haven’t verified.

Say it once, calmly

Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no urgency. Steps start with a verb.

We write
  • Check the final price at checkout.
  • Twelve unworn pieces with tags, reduced by at least 30%.
  • Rules change: check them again before you buy.
We don’t write
  • Don’t miss these INSANE deals!!
  • Use code WELCOME10 for 10% off
  • The ultimate must-have bag of the season

08 · Applications

One layout, every channel.

Wordmark top-left, uppercase label, serif headline with one italic word, short rule, dash list, uppercase footer. Scale the same system to each format.

Every channel carries the disclosure: “Affiliate link” next to store links, and the full sentence in footers and captions.

Never show brand promo codes. Never use a store’s logo or photos as our own visuals.
